About

Ten years behind the switcher, the playback rig and the production desk. From advisory boards to arena stages.

AV360 Media is the company of Bryan Horton, a freelance AV technician and video operator with more than 10 years in live events.

Most of his work is as a technical operator for production companies, covering video switching, graphics, playback and media server positions at shows around the world. For a small group of direct clients, AV360 Media fully produces the event, from planning and crew through show day.
